WebDining

Universidad de La Sabana

Skip the line. Order smarter.

WebDining is a web application designed to make food ordering faster and easier for students, professors and staff at Universidad de La Sabana.

Discover WebDining

The Problem

During peak hours, university restaurants experience long waiting lines. This can cause late arrivals to classes and meetings, overcrowded hallways, slower service and inefficient restaurant workflows.

Our Solution

Digital Menu

Browse restaurant menus and choose meals directly from your device.

Digital Payment

Pay digitally without having to wait in line to place your order.

Order Tracking

Follow your order status from reception to pickup.

QR Pickup

Receive a QR code and use it to verify your order when it is ready.
